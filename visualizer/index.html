      Palette

      Bind any colour prop to a palette role ({primary}, {secondary}, {accent} …) from the inspector. From cover pulls the roles out of the track artwork. Pick Custom to freeze the palette into the preset, then click the swatches to edit it — the preset then carries its own roles block. dyn always follows the artwork.

      Manage presets

      Tick the presets you want gone, then Remove. Removed defaults stay removed on the TV until you bring them back.
